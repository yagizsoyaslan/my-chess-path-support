My Chess Path Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Introduction

My Chess Path ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use the App.

Information We Collect

My Chess Path does not require you to create an account or provide personal information directly to us.

The App may use third-party services that automatically collect certain technical and usage information, such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, app usage information, and diagnostic data.

Advertising

My Chess Path uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, and app interaction information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

Where required, the App requests the appropriate permissions and consent before using information for personalized advertising.

In-App Purchases

My Chess Path offers a one-time "Remove Ads" in-app purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

How Information Is Used

Information collected by the App and its third-party service providers may be used to provide and operate the App, display and measure advertisements, maintain app functionality, detect technical problems, and improve the App.

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services, including Google AdMob, which may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

My Chess Path is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed in connection with the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or My Chess Path, please contact the developer through the contact information associated with the App.
