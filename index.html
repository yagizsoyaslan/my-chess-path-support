About My Chess Path

My Chess Path is a chess game for iOS. The app includes optional advertisements and a one-time purchase to remove ads.

Remove Ads

The Remove Ads purchase disables advertisements in the app. If you reinstall the app, you can use the Restore Purchases option to restore your purchase.
